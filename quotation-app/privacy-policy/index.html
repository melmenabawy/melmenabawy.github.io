Privacy Policy

Quotation Manager · Effective date: August 7, 2026

1. Information Stored in the App

Quotation Manager stores the business information you enter directly into the app, including company details you create (name, contact information, addresses, logo, tax settings), customer details (names, contacts, addresses), contacts and addresses associated with your companies and customers, quotation information (line items, pricing, quantities, taxes, discounts, and notes), catalog and price-list data you configure, and app preferences and settings.

2. Local Storage

All of this information is stored locally on your device by the app. Quotation Manager does not transmit this information to, or store it on, any servers operated by the developer. The developer has no ability to view, access, or retrieve your business data.

3. PDF Generation and Sharing

Quotation Manager generates PDF quotation documents locally on your device. Sharing or exporting a PDF only happens when you choose to share it, using the standard iOS share sheet. Once you share a PDF with another app, service, or contact, that recipient handles the file under its own privacy practices, which are not covered by this policy.

4. Purchases and Subscriptions

Quotation Manager Pro is offered as an auto-renewable subscription, purchased and managed entirely through Apple's App Store using StoreKit. Purchases are processed by Apple; the developer does not receive your full payment card details. Apple may process your purchase, account, and subscription information under Apple's own privacy policy. The app receives only the subscription status and entitlement information needed to unlock Pro features.

Because subscriptions are managed by Apple, your Quotation Manager Pro entitlement can be restored on another device signed in with the same Apple ID, using the in-app Restore Purchases option. This entitlement restoration is separate from your business data; your companies, customers, and quotations are not synced between devices by Quotation Manager.

5. Tracking, Analytics, and Advertising

Quotation Manager does not show advertising, does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and does not currently use any analytics or telemetry SDK. The app does not sell your personal data.

6. Backups

Because your business data is stored locally by the app, it may be included in backups you make of your device — for example, an iCloud device backup or a local computer backup — depending on your own iOS/iPadOS and Apple ID settings. This backup behavior is controlled by Apple and your device configuration, not by any developer-operated cloud synchronization service. Quotation Manager itself does not sync your business data between devices.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Your business data remains stored locally until you delete it yourself within the app, remove it through app functionality, or erase your device or its storage. Uninstalling the app may remove its locally stored data from your device, subject to Apple's own backup and restore behavior.

8. Children's Privacy

Quotation Manager is a general-purpose business tool intended for business owners and professionals, and it is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from children, and the app is not intended for use by children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected by an updated effective date above, and continued use of the app after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy. Within the app, an updated legal document may require your acceptance again before continued use.

10. Contact

You can reach the developer using the contact details provided on Quotation Manager's official App Store listing.
