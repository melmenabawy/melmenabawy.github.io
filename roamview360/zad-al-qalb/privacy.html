Zad Al-Qalb Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

What the app does

Zad Al-Qalb is a Muslim prayer companion for learning Salah, prayer times, Qibla direction, Fajr alarms, Adhan support, reminders, widgets, and Apple Watch features.

Data collection

Zad Al-Qalb has no developer-operated account system and does not collect, sell, or share personal data for advertising. We do not use tracking, analytics, or advertising services.

Location

Prayer times and Qibla use location on your device. Your precise location is not stored on our servers or shared with us. Apple system services may process location information for features such as place names and location search.

Apple and system services

The app may contact Apple services for App Store version or update checks. Widgets and Apple Watch features use Apple on-device and system mechanisms such as App Groups and WatchConnectivity.

Support communications

If you choose to use the in-app Prayer Time Report / Send Report feature, your report is user-initiated and may be shared using Mail or the iOS Share Sheet. Contact us at support@roamview360.com.

Children

Zad Al-Qalb is a general-audience app rated 4+. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app or its practices change. The updated date above will show when changes are made.
